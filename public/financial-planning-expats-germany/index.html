Holistic Financial Planning for Expats in Germany

Moving to Germany resets almost every part of your financial life at once — pension, taxes, investing, insurance, and eventually property and family planning. Here's how the pieces fit together.

Most financial advice you'll find online is written for people who grew up in the system it describes. As an expat, you're often navigating German pension rules, German tax residency, and German investment taxation for the first time, in a second language, while also managing whatever financial ties you kept in your home country.

None of these pieces exist in isolation. Your tax residency status affects how your investments are taxed. Your pension gap affects how aggressively you should be investing. Your marital status affects your inheritance tax exposure. A plan that only looks at one piece at a time tends to miss the interactions that actually matter.
