Niall Bradfield

FOUNDER · THRIVE ABROAD · STUTTGART

Moving to a new country shouldn't mean starting your financial life from zero. That's why Thrive Abroad exists. I've seen how many highly capable people — engineers, developers, researchers, founders — relocate to Germany and get left to figure out taxes, pensions and investing on their own, in a language and system they didn't grow up with.

The answer isn't more products sold or more confusing insurance contracts. The answer is clarity. Real numbers. Formulas you can actually follow. No fine print.

Thrive Abroad is the tool I wish existed when I started advising expats: calculators that explain rather than sell. That show the real math — pension gaps, inflation, tax optimization, compound interest — not a simplified version of it. Everything transparent, everything verifiable.

As an independent financial advisor based in Stuttgart, I've spent the last several years personally advising clients on German pension law, taxes and long-term investing — 100+ people so far. The most common reaction after our first conversation: "Why did nobody explain this to me before?" That's what keeps me doing this.
